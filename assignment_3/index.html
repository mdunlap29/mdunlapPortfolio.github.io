Directions

Our cafe is located right near the intersection of W Baltimore Pike and Union Street. Just walk down W Baltimore Pike, following the same direction as the cars driving on the road. Keep walking until you see Forage Books and Cafe Emis. Our cafe is located right between the two stores and should be easy to spot with out signage on the outside of our storefront. Stop in anytime during our store hours!
