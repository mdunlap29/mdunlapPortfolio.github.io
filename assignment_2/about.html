Our Story

Mack's Records is an independent record store built with the purpose of providing everyone any record they could imagine. We wanted to create a community for music lovers to meet and expand their love of music. And we belive that listening to music on records is a special experience that helps with both those goals.

Our collection ranges from classic rock, to jazz, to pop, to soul, and to newer genres too! Whether you want to start collecting or are adding to your collection, Mack's Records is the place for you. And with the vast genres available, you are always able to discover new music that might turn into your new favorites!

Our Mission

Creating community for music lovers!
